Why Choose SQI College of ICT?

At SQI College of ICT, we are dedicated to nurturing the next generation of tech leaders. Our National Diploma (ND) programmes are designed to provide students with the practical skills and knowledge needed to excel in the rapidly evolving tech industry.

With a focus on 80% hands-on training, our students are not only prepared for the job market but also equipped to secure remote jobs even before graduation.

Get the skills

SQI integrates quality, convenience and modern technology into education, bridging the gap between theory and real-world application.

Get the certificate

The National Diploma (ND) offered at SQI College of ICT is a two-year approved academic programme of the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE).

Get the job

Students develop practical skills and prepare for employment opportunities after graduation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SQI deviating from its core ICT focus? ⌄

No! SQI is taking ICT where it belongs. The function of ICT is to empower other industries and should not be standalone. Every industry today requires knowledge of ICT, and schools should no longer be delivering any course without it. This is what SQI is pioneering. Our accredited Accountancy and Business Administration courses integrate ICT.

Is the school accredited, and if so, by what accrediting body? ⌄

The National Diploma programme is described as an approved academic programme of the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE). Contact Admissions for current details.

Can I obtain a degree certificate at SQI? ⌄

Contact the Admissions team for information about qualifications available for your chosen programme.

Do the HND students go for NYSC? ⌄

Eligibility depends on the qualification and applicable NYSC requirements. Please confirm with the school.

Can I apply online? ⌄

Visit the official SQI website or contact Admissions for the current application process.

How much is the school fees? ⌄

Tuition fees may vary by programme and academic session. Contact Admissions for the latest fee schedule.

Does the college go on strike? ⌄
